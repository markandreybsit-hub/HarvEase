Our Story

Post-harvest work shouldn't cost farmers their morning

Harv-Ease started with a simple observation: small-scale farmers in Muntinlupa lose hours every harvest day to sorting, counting and packing — time that could go back into the field. So we built a mobile crew that shows up at the plot with crates, scales and labels, and finishes the prep work on site.

The result is produce that moves from soil to buyer in a single day, properly graded and clearly labeled, without the farmer ever leaving the farm.

  • Founded by five BSBA Major in HRDM students based in Muntinlupa City.
  • Aligned with the UN SDGs and Muntinlupa's 7K Agenda
  • Every crate carries a batch code back to the farm and picker
